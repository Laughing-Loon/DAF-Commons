The Problem:

DAF holders, like us & maybe you, steward $326 billion in the U.S.. And with 25% of philanthropic capital flowing to DAFs from the Great Wealth Transfer, that is likely to grow exponentially over the next decade to perhaps $4.5 trillion. Yet the majority of DAF holders capital is invested conventionally, not maliciously, but without impact alignment.
